## Leader notes after T03c
- Across T03, T03b, T03c, T03d and Enes's OPT FEA, pole changing helps **only the IM** (cage rotor adapts at no cost); every synchronous rotor gains nothing because its field is already adjustable or its 4-pole flux is yoke-capped. Expect the surviving gap to be IM-centred (H01/H02/H05) or non-efficiency (H06 transition, H08 fault tolerance), unless H03 changes the synchronous picture.
- Spin-off (not pole changing): SPB + HESM with a small PM share (s ≈ 0.25) gives WRSM-class cruise with half the field loss — park as side result.
