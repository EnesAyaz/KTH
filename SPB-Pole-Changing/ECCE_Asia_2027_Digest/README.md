# ECCE Asia 2027 design selection digest

## Active paper
**Joint Selection of GaN SPB Cells and Pole-Changing Windings for Truck Propulsion**

The current deliverable is `ECCE_Asia_2027_Digest_Numbered_Equations.docx`, built from the official conference Word template. The main study now selects a cell count and pole combination from device and winding constraints. It does not prescribe24 cells or12/6 poles in advance.

Three counts must be distinguished:
1. Demonstrated stator-control minimum: two three-phase cells for the explicit36-slot12/6 example in the tested family.
2. Device-voltage floor:20,14,10,4 cells for100,150,200,650 V ratings under the stated1320 V maximum-link stress scenario.
3. Actual feasible cell count after a fixed winding/slot and pole-pair layout is imposed. This can exceed the voltage floor.

All cells are three wire; their internal floating neutral points are not interconnected. There are3N inverter phase branches but only2N independent instantaneous current coordinates after KCL constraints. Balanced currents, balanced winding EMFs and equal mean loading are distinct checks.

## Literature basis and preliminary result
Verified production examples include48 slots/8 total poles for Toyota Prius and Nissan LEAF, and72 slots/12 total poles for BMW i3. They are useful anchors, not a claim about global market shares. A universal40- or60-slot EV ceiling is unsupported. The paper uses60 slots as a design scenario and72 as a sensitivity.

The tested balanced family supports2:1 and5:2. It does not demonstrate3:1; this is not a universal impossibility result. A2:1 ratio is a strong starting case because of the short-pitch utilization and existing hybrid-rotor polarity-change concept. A5:2 stator design needs longer pitch for its largest factor and an independently validated rotor.

No global efficiency/cost optimum is claimed. Candidate priorities are150–200 V versus100 V, after device loss, thermal, winding and driver costs are modeled. The350 V example demonstrates why fewer cells alone need not win: the verified EPC2050 has much higher on-resistance and a different current/package scale than the other example parts.
