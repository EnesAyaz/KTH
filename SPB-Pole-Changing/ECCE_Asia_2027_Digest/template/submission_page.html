Digest Submission Guidelines
1. All digests must be written in clear English and submitted in PDF file no later than November 22, 2026 (23:59 KST, UTC+9).
2. A digest template is provided for the preparation of your digest. (Template Download-CLICK)
3. Authors may further edit and modify submitted digests until the submission deadline via My Page.
4. Digests must not have been published or submitted for presentation at any national and/or international meeting before ICPE 2027-ECCE Asia.
5. Authors are required to indicate their presentation preference (oral, poster, or either) by checking the appropriate box on the submission web page. The Technical Program Committee, however, reserves the right to make the final decision.
6. An acknowledgment email will be sent to all authors after submission.
Important: If you do not receive an acknowledgment email, your digest may not have been successfully submitted.
7. Authors will be notified of acceptance by January 15, 2027.
8. Authors of accepted digests will be required to submit a final manuscript that should be strictly no fewer than 6 pages, and preferably no more than 8 pages in length.
9. Papers will not be included in the ICPE 2027-ECCE Asia proceedings if they do not have a fully paid registration associated with them.
10. All papers presented at the conference will be included in IEEE Xplore.
 
Self-citation Percentage Check
When submitting the digest, the corresponding author must ensure that the percentage of self-citations in the final paper does not exceed 25%.
 
Registration Policy
• Papers will be included in the ICPE 2027–ECCE Asia proceedings only if a fully paid registration is completed by March 31, 2027.
• To be scheduled in the technical program, each accepted paper must be associated with one Regular Rate registration, completed by one of the paper’s authors, regardless of academic status or the number of papers accepted for that author.
• Each full registration covers one (1) paper.
• Authors submitting two or more papers will be required to pay an additional student registration fee for each extra paper submitted by the same author.
 
Final Manuscripts Submission
• Authors of accepted papers are required to submit a final manuscript with a length of no fewer than six (6) pages, and preferably no more than eight (8) pages.
• Detailed instructions for final manuscript preparation and submission will be provided via e-mail to authors whose digests have been accepted.
• Final Manuscript Submission Deadline: March 12, 2027
 
Review Format
Item Description
Title Ensure that the title is concise, clearly conveys the subject, and does not exceed 30 Words. Use Title Case (capitalize the first letter of each major word).
Authors Do not include author names (omitted for peer review).
Affiliations Do not include author affiliations (omitted for peer review).
Content Clearly and concisely describe the problem addressed, the proposed work and its contribution, the methods used, and specific results. Include evidence of completed experimental work whenever possible.
Images Allowed. Evidence of completed experiments, such as oscillograms and photographs of test equipment, is highly valued by reviewers.
Format A4 size (210 × 297 mm), single column, single-spaced (line spacing adjustable up to 1.5). Minimum 10 pt font. Margins: top 3 cm, bottom 1.65cm, left/right 1.65cm. All submissions must be in PDF format.
Page Limit Maximum 5 pages, including figures and tables.
Page Numbering Page numbers must be included on all pages.
Originality The digest must contain original, unpublished work that has not been previously presented or published.
Confidentiality Digests marked or labeled as “Confidential,” “Proprietary,” or similar will not be accepted.
Export Control & Clearance All digests must comply with the export control laws of the country or countries in which the authors reside. A statement of compliance is required, and company and governmental clearance must be obtained prior to submission.
 
Acceptance Notification
January 15, 2027
• All submitted digests will be reviewed by the technical program committee according to standard review procedures.
• Acceptance notifications will be emailed to corresponding, presenting, and digest-submitting authors by January 15, 2027.
• If you have not received the notification, please send an email to the secretariat (secretary@icpe-conf.org) with your digest information.
 
Paper Withdrawal
January 15, 2027
• If the presenting author of an accepted digest does not submit their final manuscript by submission due date, the digest will be automatically withdrawn from the final program.
• If you need to withdraw the digest, please notify the secretariat (secretary@icpe-conf.org) by email no later than January 15, 2027.
