# Filippini2024design

**Design of an On-Axis Electrically Excited Synchronous Motor for Heavy Commercial Vehicles** (2024), 2024 IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition (ECCE). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ecce55643.2024.10861426

Access: abstract + full text read (IEEE PDF supplied by Enes).

- **Claim:** On-axis EESM design for heavy commercial vehicles with heavy-duty-truck drive cycle, showing the benefit of optimal rotor excitation current control.
- **Method:** Design process and drive-cycle evaluation.
- **Machine / inverter:** EESM, heavy truck (Bianchi).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** See full-text addition below.
- **Full-text addition (corrects abstract-level and secondary numbers):** On-axis EESM, 800 V, 510 kW peak (overload), 10 krpm, 6 pole pairs, 72 slots, OD 400 mm, ID 338 mm, stack 120 mm, shaft 230 mm, airgap 1.1 mm, slot fill 0.65, rotor 310 turns, rated rotor current 4.4 A (rotor RMS over the cycle 1.1 A = 63 W). Vehicle: 35 t with 2 motors, wheel radius 0.475 m, gear ratio 19, gear efficiency 0.94, Cd 0.53, A 9 m2, Cr 0.0051. CHTC-TT cycle: peak power requested 448 kW for a short time; motor mostly in the high-speed low-torque region; machine efficiency close to 97 % most of the time with optimal rotor current, over 95 % across a wide speed range; optimal excitation reduces stator current and loss.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Truck EESM drive-cycle reference with I_f optimisation, i.e. the no-pole-change WRSM baseline for D02.
- **Limitations:** Full text now read (see addition). Prototype or FEA scale; no truck-rating machine.
- **SPB note (from full text):** Gives a 97 % cycle-typical efficiency for a 510 kW-class EESM with optimal I_f; supports a high cruise efficiency for a WRSM but is machine-only (no inverter, no gear).
