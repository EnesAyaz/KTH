- **Method:** 2D FEA.
- **Machine / inverter:** Two inverters, 3-layer stator winding, 8-pole 48-slot.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** none in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Same 8-pole 48-slot geometry class as the analyst's order-8 screen; sub-harmonic is excited as an independent space order by inverter sets.
- **Limitations:** abstract only; small machines, FEA mostly; unipolar field current.
