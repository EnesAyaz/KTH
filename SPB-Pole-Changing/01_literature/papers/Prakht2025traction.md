# Prakht2025traction

**Traction Synchronous Machine with Rotor Field Winding and Two-Phase Harmonic Field Exciter** (2025), World Electric Vehicle Journal. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj16010025

Access: abstract + full text read.

- **Claim:** Brushless traction WRSM with a two-phase harmonic exciter winding on the rotor and zero-sequence injection.
- **Method:** Simplified FE-based steady-state model; hysteresis vs field-oriented PI current controllers.
- **Machine / inverter:** Rotor field winding + 2-phase harmonic exciter; stator zero-sequence injection.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Introduction: a 50 kW IM with CPSR 3.5 needs a 125 kVA inverter (utilisation 0.4); same for CPSR 4:1 (citing other work).
- **Relevance to SPB:** The inverter-oversizing figure is a quantitative motivation for extending CPSR by electronic means. Zero-sequence injection pairs naturally with isolated neutrals.
- **Limitations:** Exciter concept, not a full traction rating.
