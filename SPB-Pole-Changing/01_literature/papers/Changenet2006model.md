# Changenet2006model

**A Model for the Prediction of Churning Losses in Geared Transmissions—Preliminary Results** (2006), Journal of Mechanical Design. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1115/1.2403727

Access: abstract record read.

- **Method:** Experimental validation.
- **Machine / inverter:** Automotive-transmission geometry pinion.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** See Yan2024power for the working formula.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Basis of the churning term.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only.
