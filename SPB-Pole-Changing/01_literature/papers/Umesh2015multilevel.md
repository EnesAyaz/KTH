# Umesh2015multilevel

**Multilevel Inverter Scheme for Performance Improvement of Pole Phase Modulated Multiphase Induction Motor Drive (TIE 2015)**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tie.2015.2506623

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Carrier phase-shifted multilevel voltage across the equal-voltage-profile coils of a 9-phase PPM IM, no extra switches.
- **Method:** FEM co-simulation, nine-phase cage IM, two-level 9-phase inverter.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 9 phases; speed ratio 1:3.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Shows that in high-pole modes the coil groups of a PPM machine can be phase-shifted to act as a multilevel inverter: the same mechanism SPB uses for series stacking.
- **Limitations:** Simulation / small lab; one DC link.
