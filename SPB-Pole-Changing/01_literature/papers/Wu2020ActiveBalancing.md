- **Claim:** Active voltage balancing of an integrated modular drive with series DC-link capacitors, megawatt-level for MEA.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** In order to reduce the size and weight of transmission cables, high direct current (DC) bus voltage is desired for electrical machine drives used in the hybrid powertrains of more-electric aircraft (MEA). An integrated modular machine drive (IMMD) with DC-link capacitors connected in series is adopted to break down the supply voltage for each drive unit. With lower electrical stress, partial discharge (PD) risks at high altitudes can be significantly reduced. This paper deals with the challenge of active DC-link voltage balancing control in a megawatt-level integrated modular machine drives. V
