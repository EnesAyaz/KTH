# Hsu2004report

**Report on Toyota/Prius Motor Torque-Capability, Torque-Property, No-Load Back EMF, and Mechanical Losses** (2004), OSTI report. DOI: https://doi.org/10.2172/885669

Access: report PDF read (OSTI).

- **Claim:** Measured mechanical losses of the 2004 Toyota Prius motor and gear system: at 6000 rpm and 30 kW load the total mechanical loss (gears + rotor + planetary) is 2.4 kW, a 7.4 % efficiency drop; losses fall with lower speed.
- **Method:** Dynamometer measurement at ORNL; losses plotted versus motor shaft speed (Fig. 15, values not tabulated).
- **Machine / inverter:** 50 kW peak, 400 N m, 1040-5600 rpm constant power; with planetary gear set.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 2.4 kW mechanical loss at 6000 rpm incl. gears; back EMF 500 V at 3600 rpm and 850 V at 6000 rpm.
- **Relevance to SPB:** A measured mechanical-loss calibration point for a 50 kW motor with gears: scale-up needed for 100-400 kW.
- **Limitations:** 50 kW, not 100-400 kW; motor-only part not separated in the numbers read; no ordinary-motor friction curve extracted.
