# Fernandes2015power

**Power loss prediction: Application to a 2.5 MW wind turbine gearbox** (2015), Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, Part J: Journal of Engineering Tribology. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/1350650115622362

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Gear plus rolling-bearing power-loss model validated on test rigs, applied to a 2.5 MW wind-turbine gearbox.
- **Method:** Calibrated loss models.
- **Machine / inverter:** Wind-turbine planetary gearbox.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 2.5 MW.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Calibration approach for gear and bearing models.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only; wind scale.
