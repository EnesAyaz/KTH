# Sirewal2024dual

DOI: https://doi.org/10.23919/icems60997.2024.10921400

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Dual-inverter open-end-winding brushless WRSM: two inverters supply three-phase currents plus a zero-sequence third-harmonic current, creating a harmonic field in the air gap that induces voltage in a rotor harmonic winding (also at standstill as a pulsating field); rectifier feeds the field winding.
- **Method:** 2D FEA (Maxwell).
- **Machine / inverter:** Open-end winding, two inverters (isolated neutral is replaced by two bridges).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** none in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Open-end dual inverter is the nearest hardware analogue of isolated-neutral modules, but injects zero-sequence third harmonic, not an independent space order.
- **Limitations:** abstract only; small machines, FEA mostly; unipolar field current.
