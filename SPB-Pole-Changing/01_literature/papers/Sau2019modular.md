# Sau2019modular

**Modular Multilevel Converter Based Variable Speed Drive With Reduced Capacitor Ripple Voltage** (2019), IEEE TIE 66(5), 3412-3421. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tie.2018.2860542

Access: abstract record read; full text not read.

- **Claim:** MMC drives have large sub-module capacitor ripple at low speed; usual remedy is a high-frequency circulating current in each arm; this paper lowers the DC-bus voltage at low speed instead.
- **Relevance to SPB:** the standard 'low-frequency module power pulsation' result: arm current and arm voltage at nearly the same low frequency make cell power pulsate at that frequency. It is a single-order, single-frequency effect and is cured by common-mode or circulating injection; the SPB effect comes from two simultaneous spatial orders in different phase planes and cannot use that cure.
- **Limitations:** abstract only; MMC medium-voltage drive.
