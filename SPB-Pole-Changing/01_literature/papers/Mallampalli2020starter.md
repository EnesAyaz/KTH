# Mallampalli2020starter

**48 V Starter-Generator Induction Machine With Pole-Changing Windings** (2020), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2020.3012615

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** 8/4-pole electronic pole-changing IM for a 48 V starter-generator; shorter stack than baseline IM.
- **Method:** Analytical and FE efficiency, loss and power-factor maps; experimental validation.
- **Machine / inverter:** 48 V class, 8/4-pole IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 48 V.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Same low-voltage regime as an SPB module; shows compaction benefit.
- **Limitations:** Starter-generator, not traction power.
