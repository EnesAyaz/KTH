# Chaubal2024continuously

**Continuously-Variable-Pole Induction Machine Drive for Electric Vehicles (2024), ITEC**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/itec60657.2024.10598847

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Continuous pole sharing: 2-pole plus 6-pole torque components simultaneously give minimum loss at intermediate torque-speed points.
- **Method:** Optimisation of torque sharing, common-mode-injection modulation, one pole under vector and one under scalar control; experiment on 36-slot toroidal machine.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 36 slots, 18-leg drive; no truck numbers.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Shows that blending poles is an additional efficiency degree of freedom beyond discrete pole selection; relevant to H04/H05 (mode scheduling).
- **Limitations:** IM only; lab scale.
