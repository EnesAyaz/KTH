# ISO14179

**ISO/TR 14179-1/-2:2001 Gears - Thermal capacity** (2001), ISO. No DOI (see refs.bib url)

Access: UNVERIFIED content (standard paywalled; catalogue/summary pages only).

- **Claim:** ISO/TR 14179 gives gear-drive thermal capacity and power-loss calculations including no-load (churning, windage) and load-dependent losses; part 2 is the thermal-equilibrium method with churning loss formulas.
- **Method:** Heat balance of gear drives.
- **Machine / inverter:** Gearboxes.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Not read. Practical gear loss formulas (Ohlendorf meshing and windage, Changenet churning) are written out in Yan2024power.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Reference standard for the churning/no-load model: cite with caution.
- **Limitations:** Content not accessed.
