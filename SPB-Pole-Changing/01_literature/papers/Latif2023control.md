- **Claim:** Control of a 4/2-pole electronic pole-changing IM for traction: flux of the incoming pole is stabilised before the torque command changes; smooth transition with minimum torque transient.

- **Machine / inverter:** 4-pole/2-pole IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Enhanced constant-power region versus baseline; no numbers in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Experimental proof of loaded pole transition in traction IM.
