# Bocker2025minimizing

**Tags: own-work**

**Minimizing Losses in Electric Drivetrains: A Comparative Analysis of Inverter Topologies, Switching Frequencies, and Modulation Techniques** (2025), 2025 IEEE International Electric Machines \&amp; Drives Conference (IEMDC). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/iemdc60492.2025.11061021

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Switching frequency and modulation vs drivetrain efficiency for PMSM heavy vehicles, 2L vs 3L inverters at long-haul operating points.
- **Method:** Current-based FEA machine loss + virtual-prototyping inverter loss.
- **Machine / inverter:** PMSM + 2L/3L inverter, long-haul truck key points. Coauthors Ayaz and Peretti (KTH).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** An optimal switching frequency exists; 3L gives notably lower system loss.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Template for the system-level (inverter+machine) comparison needed for SPB at long-haul operating points.
- **Limitations:** PMSM only; no pole changing.
