# Dmitrievskii2023comparative

**Comparative Study of Induction and Wound Rotor Synchronous Motors for the Traction Drive of a Mining Dump Truck Operating in Wide Constant Power Speed Range** (2023), IEEE Access. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3292244

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** WRSM vs IM for a 90 t mining dump truck with CPSR 1:10: WRSM lighter, lower current, lower losses, and no low-frequency inverter thermal cycling when holding on a slope.
- **Method:** Nelder-Mead optimisation; 2D time-domain FEA for the IM; same stator OD.
- **Machine / inverter:** WRSM (nine-phase winding per Prakht2025review) vs IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Secondary, via Prakht2025review: 370 kW, CPSR 1:10 (400 to 4000 rpm), max torque 8833 N m, stator OD 668 mm; WRSM active weight -15 %, phase current 1.3-1.5x lower, losses 9-24 % lower.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Extreme-CPSR traction case with a multiphase WRSM; motivation for widening CPSR electronically.
- **Limitations:** Mining truck, off-highway; numbers secondary.
