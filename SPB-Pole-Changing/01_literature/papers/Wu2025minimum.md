# Wu2025minimum

**Minimum Torque Dip Pole Transition in Variable Phase-Pole Machines With Voltage Limitation** (2025), IEEE Transactions on Energy Conversion. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tec.2024.3489225

Access: abstract + full text read (KTH DiVA postprint).

- **Claim:** Pole-transition strategy that minimises the torque dip, with field weakening added for the voltage limit.
- **Method:** Experimental scheduled, loaded pole transitions with and without voltage limit.
- **Machine / inverter:** Variable phase-pole IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** None in abstract.
- **Full-text addition:** Experiments: the same 15 kW ABB M3BP 160MLB 2 IE2 induction motor (Qs = 36, Qr = 28, 15 kW, 400 V, 26 A, 50 Hz, 2934 rpm, 48.8 N m) with open-end winding arranged as 3/6/9/12 phases; scheduled pole transitions at 23.5 N m and 750 rpm, with and without a 100 V voltage limit. Torque dip in the table of related strategies: hard switch 133 % torque drop, PPM 83 %, GEPC 100 % oscillations, premagnetisation 50 %, proposed 10 % (and <7.5 % in the voltage-limited variant).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Addresses the pole-transition torque gap that remains open for SPB over a drive cycle.
- **Limitations:** Lab scale.
- **SPB note (from full text):** The 10 % / <7.5 % torque dip is the transition cost number the SPB drive-cycle study needs; also states that VPP machines can be more efficient than PMSMs at higher speed and that IMs can match PMSM efficiency over vehicle drive cycles (citing Buyukdegirmenci2014evaluation).
