# Changenet2011note

**A Note on Flow Regimes and Churning Loss Modeling** (2011), Journal of Mechanical Design. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4005330

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Extends churning loss formulas with centrifugal effects for wide-faced gears and high temperatures; churning and windage mechanisms differ.
- **Method:** New transient measurements.
- **Machine / inverter:** Gears partly immersed in oil.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** None in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Refinement for high speed e-axle gears.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only.
