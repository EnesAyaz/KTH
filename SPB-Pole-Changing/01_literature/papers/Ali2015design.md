# Ali2015design

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tmag.2015.2440433

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Brushless WRSM excited by an additional sub-harmonic component of the stator MMF generated by a dual-inverter stator winding; a rotor excitation winding feeds the field winding through a rotating rectifier.
- **Method:** 2D FEA.
- **Machine / inverter:** Dual-inverter stator, rotor excitation winding + field winding + rotating rectifier. Per Rafin2023subharmonic the design family is 8-pole, 48-slot with a fundamental four times the sub-harmonic MMF.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** none in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Closest prior art to H03: an independent stator space order (sub-harmonic) made by multiple inverters, harvested by a rotor winding and rectified. Unipolar field, two inverters, no isolated-neutral modules, no pole changing.
- **Limitations:** abstract only; small machines, FEA mostly; unipolar field current.
