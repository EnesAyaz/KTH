# Reddy2020multilayer

**Multilayer Fractional Slot Pole-Phase Modulated Induction Motor Drives for Traction Applications (TIE 2020)**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tie.2019.2958287

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Fractional-slot multilayer winding removes the high-slot-count requirement of PPM: a 15-phase winding in 36 slots runs three pole-phase combinations (1:3:5 speed ratio) for EV and locomotive use.
- **Method:** Winding design, carrier-phase-shifted PWM with phase grouping, validation (abstract).
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 15 phases, 36 slots, 1:3:5.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Gives the slot-count versus (m, p) constraint that H01 needs as design rules.
- **Limitations:** IM; small.
