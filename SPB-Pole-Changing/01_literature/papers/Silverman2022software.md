# Silverman2022software

**A Software-Defined Stacked Multilevel Motor Drive Inverter with Linear Component Scaling** (2022), 2022 IEEE Transportation Electrification Conference \& Expo (ITEC). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/itec53557.2022.9813833

Access: abstract + full text read.

- **Claim:** Series-stacked cells of low-voltage FETs with local feedback form a software-defined multilevel motor inverter; fast, low-R_on switches become usable at high bus voltage.
- **Method:** 5-cell converter simulated, dynamic cell-level model; each cell = FETs + filtering + local control.
- **Machine / inverter:** Three-phase motor drive inverter, 5-level (5 cells).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 2.5 kW simulated drive, 300 Hz torque-control bandwidth. Stated drawback: large inductor currents near the centre of the stack. No hardware efficiency.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Closest published analogue of a stacked low-voltage-device traction inverter. SPB differs: each module feeds its own isolated-neutral coil group instead of synthesising one multilevel phase voltage.
- **Limitations:** Simulation only, 2.5 kW, one three-phase machine; no pole changing, no drive cycle.
