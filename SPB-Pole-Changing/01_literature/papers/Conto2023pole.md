# Conto2023pole

**Pole-Changing in Synchronous Machines: A Reluctance-Permanent Magnet Hybrid Motor** (2023), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2023.3338194

Access: abstract + full text read (IEEE PDF supplied by Enes).

- **Claim:** Pole changing in synchronous machines: hybrid reluctance-permanent-magnet motor with dual-polarity operation verified on a prototype.
- **Method:** FE analysis plus prototype tests. The ICEM 2022 conference version (Conto2022dual) states the idea: when the stator pole number changes in a synchronous machine, the rotor pole number must be changed accordingly.
- **Machine / inverter:** Hybrid reluctance-PM synchronous motor; mechanism of rotor pole change not available in the abstract.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** See full-text addition below.
- **Full-text addition (corrects abstract-level and secondary numbers):** Reluctance-PM synchronous motor with Dahlander-type stator (24 slots, six terminals A,B,C,X,Y,Z): star-star gives 8 poles, delta gives 4 poles; the rotor is hybrid: eight inset SmCo PMs make the 8-pole machine, while with the 4-pole stator the PM flux is not linked and the four iron salient edges form a 4-pole reluctance rotor (reluctance path 22 % and magnet surface 73 % of rotor surface). Prototype tested with current up to 5 A and 350 V DC bus; efficiency map: 8-pole region above 90 % only between 900 and 1200 r/min at 2-4 N m, above 80 % over a wider area; 4-pole reluctance mode peak efficiency about 50 %, speed range to 3500 r/min; high torque ripple. Pole change by contactor-style winding reconnection (wye to delta).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Bianchi group's second pole-changing synchronous machine; confirms that a synchronous machine needs matching rotor poles.
- **Limitations:** Full text now read (see addition). Prototype or FEA scale; no truck-rating machine.
- **SPB note (from full text):** Shows the cost of switching PMs off by reluctance: 4-pole mode about 50 % efficiency. A PM synchronous machine can pole change only at the price of a reluctance mode; relevant to why HESM with field coils is better than reluctance-PM.
