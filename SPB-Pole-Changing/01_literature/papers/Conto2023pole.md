# Conto2023pole

**Pole-Changing in Synchronous Machines: A Reluctance-Permanent Magnet Hybrid Motor** (2023), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2023.3338194

Access: abstract record read (leader cites TIA 2024).

- **Claim:** Pole changing is possible in synchronous machines: a hybrid reluctance-PM motor with dual-polarity operation demonstrated on a prototype.
- **Method:** FE design and operating principle, prototype tests.
- **Machine / inverter:** Hybrid reluctance-permanent-magnet synchronous motor with changeable pole number (Univ. Padova).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** No ratings in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Bianchi group's synchronous pole-changing; shows PM can be made pole-changing only with a special rotor, supporting the D01 statement that a plain PM rotor cannot.
- **Limitations:** Small prototype; no traction rating.
