# Haq2024ripple

**Ripple-Free Phase-Pole Modulation of a Multiphase Induction Machine** (2024), 2024 IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition (ECCE). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ecce55643.2024.10861520

Access: abstract + full text read downloaded from KTH DiVA; skimmed only.

- **Claim:** Synchronised phase-pole modulation removes beat oscillation during pole-pair transition and optimises stator current peaks.
- **Method:** Experiment on a 9-phase IM versus standard transitions.
- **Machine / inverter:** 9-phase IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 9 phases.
- **Full-text addition:** Experiment on a 15 kW class 9-phase IM on a common DC link fed by a 22 kW regenerative converter (lab figures read from the setup description).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Ripple-free transition method; relevant to the torque gap in the SPB cycle study.
- **Limitations:** Lab scale.
