# Michaelis2011influence

**Influence factors on gearbox power loss** (2011), Industrial Lubrication and Tribology. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/00368791111101830

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** No-load losses can be cut with low-viscosity oils and low immersion depth; overall gearbox losses can be reduced by 50 % on average.
- **Method:** Combined projects review.
- **Machine / inverter:** Gearboxes (automotive).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Up to 50 % reduction potential.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Shows scale of no-load loss and its controllability.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only.
