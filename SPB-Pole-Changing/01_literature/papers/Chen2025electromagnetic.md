# Chen2025electromagnetic

**Electromagnetic Performance Investigation of a Brushless Electrically Excited Synchronous Machine for Long-Distance Heavy-Duty Electric Vehicles** (2025), IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tte.2024.3388430

Access: abstract + full text read (IEEE PDF supplied by Enes).

- **Claim:** Brushless EESM (rotary transformer) for long-distance heavy-duty EVs: PMSM more efficient at low speed, EESM much more efficient at high speed; EESM cheaper and rare-earth free.
- **Method:** Design and quantitative FE comparison with PMSM over a duty cycle with high share of high-speed operation.
- **Machine / inverter:** Brushless EESM with rotary transformer vs IPMSM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** SUPERSEDED by the full-text addition below. Earlier Secondary, via Prakht2025review: 215 kW max, 581 N m, rated 3000 rpm, max 8000 rpm, OD 258 mm, L 200 mm; high-speed efficiency 92.46 % vs 87.61 %; cycle-average efficiency +4.11 %; power factor 0.96 vs 0.45; about 90 % of the duty cycle at high speed.
- **Full-text addition (corrects abstract-level and secondary numbers):** Truck from the EU LONGRUN project: 40 t, two machines. Operating points: (1) 0-3000 r/min peak torque for start-up on 12 % slope, 5 min; (2) 200 kW at 5000 r/min, 6 % slope at 50 km/h, 20 min; (3) high-speed cruise on flat road at 80 km/h, 200 min. Peak torque 581.33 N m (both machines). Efficiencies EESM vs PMSM: point 1 94.10 % vs 96.30 % (PF 0.86 both; rotor field current 12.59 A); point 2 (410 N m, 214.8 vs 212.4 kW) 94.19 % vs 95.85 %; point 3 (cruise, deep flux weakening) 92.46 % vs 87.61 %, PF 0.96 vs 0.45, stator current 50.19 A vs 113.60 A. Total energy efficiency over the weighted schedule: 92.65 % (EESM) vs 88.54 % (PMSM): +4.11 percentage points (absolute, not relative). Active mass 71.17 kg vs 67.47 kg. Prototypes tested back-to-back with 250 kW test setup and rotating-transformer exciter.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Best match to D02 (40 t long-haul). Shows the WRSM already wins at high speed via I_f; electronic pole changing must add value beyond that.
- **Limitations:** Full text now read; the earlier second-hand numbers (via Prakht2025review) are corrected in the full-text addition. Simulation plus prototype tests; truck model only.
- **SPB note (from full text):** Confirms: with I_f weakening the EESM wins at cruise; at the base-speed point the PMSM is 1.7-2.2 points better. Note the corrected statement: the 4.11 % is percentage points.
