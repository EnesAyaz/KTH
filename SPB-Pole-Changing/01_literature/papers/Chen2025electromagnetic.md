# Chen2025electromagnetic

**Electromagnetic Performance Investigation of a Brushless Electrically Excited Synchronous Machine for Long-Distance Heavy-Duty Electric Vehicles** (2025), IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tte.2024.3388430

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Brushless EESM (rotary transformer) for long-distance heavy-duty EVs: PMSM more efficient at low speed, EESM much more efficient at high speed; EESM cheaper and rare-earth free.
- **Method:** Design and quantitative FE comparison with PMSM over a duty cycle with high share of high-speed operation.
- **Machine / inverter:** Brushless EESM with rotary transformer vs IPMSM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Secondary, via Prakht2025review: 215 kW max, 581 N m, rated 3000 rpm, max 8000 rpm, OD 258 mm, L 200 mm; high-speed efficiency 92.46 % vs 87.61 %; cycle-average efficiency +4.11 %; power factor 0.96 vs 0.45; about 90 % of the duty cycle at high speed.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Best match to D02 (40 t long-haul). Shows the WRSM already wins at high speed via I_f; electronic pole changing must add value beyond that.
- **Limitations:** Numbers secondary; abstract read directly.
