One of the most significant and distinct challenges that future multi-megawatt electrified propulsion will face is high voltage at high altitudes, which could cause significant partial discharge and reduce the reliability of the propulsion system. Recognizing this challenge, a multi-disciplinary research team has proposed, designed, built, and preliminarily tested a 2-kV 1-MW 20,000 RPM integrated modular motor drive (IMMD) with an unprecedented weight power density of 9 kW/kg.
