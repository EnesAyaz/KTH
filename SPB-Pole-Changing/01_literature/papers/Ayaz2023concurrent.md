# Ayaz2023concurrent

**Tags: own-work**

**Concurrent Wireless Power Transfer and Motor Drive System With a Single Converter** (2023), IEEE Journal of Emerging and Selected Topics in Industrial Electronics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/jestie.2022.3182592

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** own-work. Concurrent wireless power transfer and motor drive with a single converter: GaN switching harmonics excite a series-series WPT link while the low-frequency modulated current drives the motor.
- **Method:** Analysis of the coupling between motor drive and WPT operating conditions; experiment.
- **Machine / inverter:** GaN full-bridge, SS-WPT 50 W, dc motor 500 W.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 50 W WPT, 500 W motor; contactless slip ring.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Proof that wide-bandgap carrier harmonics can power a rotating load alongside torque control: basis for P3.
- **Limitations:** Dc motor, not synchronous machine; low power.
