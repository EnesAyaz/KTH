- **Claim:** Post-fault strategy for n-phase cascaded-H-bridge motor drives: bypass damaged H-bridges and use multiphase xy degrees of freedom to keep speed range without field weakening or torque ripple, minimising xy harmonic injection; five-level five-phase experiment.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** Multiphase motor drives based on cascaded H-bridge (CHB) voltage source inverters (VSIs) offer enhanced reliability by accommodating both motor and VSI faults. The VSI faults, the most prevalent type, can be addressed by bypassing damaged H-Bridges. However, operating the VSI in this condition without an appropriate postfault strategy can increase the torque ripple and losses, and reduce the maximum motor speed.
