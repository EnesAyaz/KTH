# Runde2019iscad

**ISCAD - Design, control and car integration of a 48 volt high performance drive (2019), CES TEMS**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.30941/cestems.2019.00017

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** ISCAD system provides up to 300 kW with a safe-to-touch 48 V battery; third-generation prototype fitted in a Geely Emgrand EV.
- **Method:** Stator cage with individually driven bars; compact prototype, in-house 48 V battery.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 300 kW at 48 V (abstract).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Shows a low-voltage, many-module, traction-class drive exists; it solves the voltage problem by 48 V parallel current, SPB by stacking.
- **Limitations:** Passenger-car scale; about 6 kA DC current implied by 300 kW at 48 V (my arithmetic, not from the paper).
