# Gerber2024evaluation

**Evaluation of Pole-Changing Wound Rotor Synchronous Wind Generator Topologies** (2024), 2024 International Conference on Electrical Machines (ICEM). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/icem60801.2024.10700507

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Round-rotor field-winding topologies that let a synchronous generator run at two distinct synchronous speeds.
- **Method:** Rotor and stator topology optimisation (torque ripple, copper loss, THD), trapezium to triangular MMF waves.
- **Machine / inverter:** Pole-changing wound-rotor synchronous generator (wind).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** None in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Second precedent for WRSM pole changing through rotor-winding MMF shaping.
- **Limitations:** Wind generator, grid-connected; not traction.
