# Kampker2022design

**Design of an Electric Drive Axle for Heavy Duty Commercial Vehicles in the Long-Haul Application Under Consideration of Energy Consumption** (2022), 2022 12th International Electric Drives Production Conference (EDPC). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/edpc56367.2022.10019741

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Integrated electric drive axle for a 41 t long-haul tractor-trailer using two PMSM and a single- or two-speed gearbox, optimised for energy use over driving cycles.
- **Method:** Static driving profiles give torque and power requirements; drivetrain simulation over cycles; motor-length parameter study.
- **Machine / inverter:** Two PMSM, 1- or 2-speed gearbox.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 41 t gross weight. Specific kW/N m values are in the paper body (not extracted).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Same vehicle class as D02. The gearbox is the incumbent way to widen the speed range, which electronic pole changing would replace.
- **Limitations:** Numbers not extracted; PMSM only.
