# Sarrazin2011cascaded

**Cascaded Inverters for electric vehicles: Towards a better management of traction chain from the battery to the motor? (2011), ISIE**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/isie.2011.5984149

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Cascaded H-bridge inverters for EVs: better battery-cell management, improved waveforms, spread thermal loss sources and better fault-mode operation; AC amplitude up to 300 V from 48 V cells.
- **Method:** Numerical and experimental analysis with optimal modulation.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 48 V cells to 300 V AC.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Prior art for cell-level management and fault operation in a cascaded EV drive (H05, H08), without pole changing.
- **Limitations:** Single machine winding; no pole change.
