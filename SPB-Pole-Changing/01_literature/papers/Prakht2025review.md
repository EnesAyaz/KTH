# Prakht2025review

**Traction Synchronous Motors with Rotor Field Winding: A Literature Review** (2025), World Electric Vehicle Journal. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj16110633

Access: abstract + full text read.

- **Claim:** Review of traction WRSMs: commercial examples, comparison with other motor types, optimisation and brushless excitation.
- **Method:** Literature review with tables of commercial WRSM drives and benchmark studies.
- **Machine / inverter:** WRSM/EESM, brushed and brushless.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Renault ZOE R135 100 kW, 11 300 rpm; BMW iX3 210 kW, 400 N m; BMW i7 xDrive60 rear 360 kW, 650 N m; Rolls-Royce Spectre 550 kW total, 1075 N m; Valeo/Mahle plan 220-350 kW brushless; ZF I2SM 75-550 kW brushless. CPSR of produced WRSMs about 2:1 to 3:1; all produced WRSMs use brushes (as of the review). Truck studies: see Mademlis, Chen, Dmitrievskii entries.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Master source for WRSM numbers in D01/D02. It lists no pole-changing traction WRSM, confirming the gap.
- **Limitations:** Table 2 text extraction is garbled, so per-study CPSR and rated values are ambiguous; verify from originals.
