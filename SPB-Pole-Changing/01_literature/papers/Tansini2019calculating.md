# Tansini2019calculating

**Calculating Heavy-Duty Truck Energy and Fuel Consumption Using Correlation Formulas Derived From VECTO Simulations** (2019), SAE Technical Paper Series. DOI: https://doi.org/10.4271/2019-01-1278

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Energy breakdown of EU heavy-duty trucks from VECTO simulation data: air drag and rolling resistance account for about 70 % of the energy consumed downstream of the engine; correlation formulas give component losses (axle, gearbox) over Regional Delivery and Long Haul cycles.
- **Method:** JRC analysis of manufacturer VECTO simulation data of the 2016 fleet.
- **Machine / inverter:** Conventional diesel HD trucks.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** About 70 % aero + rolling downstream of the engine; per-component percentages are in the paper body.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Reference for the share of transmission and axle loss in a long-haul cycle.
- **Limitations:** Conventional trucks; per-component values not read.
