# Osama1997modeling

**Modeling and analysis of a wide-speed-range induction motor drive based on electronic pole changing** (1997), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/28.633794

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Contactorless pole changing of an IM by reversing the current in selected stator coil groups; compares power and torque with fixed 4-pole and 2-pole drives.
- **Method:** Vector space decomposition turns the coupled coil-group model into decoupled 4-pole and 2-pole dq0 models; transient pole-change analysis.
- **Machine / inverter:** IM with stator coil groups fed by an inverter.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Theory; no ratings in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Foundational. SPB modules driving separate coil groups reproduce exactly the coil-group scheme.
- **Limitations:** 1997; no low-voltage-device angle; assumes many inverter legs.
