# Brown2022wound

**Wound Field and Hybrid Synchronous Machines for EV Traction with Brushless Capacitive Rotor Field Excitation (Final Report)** (2022), OSTI report. DOI: https://doi.org/10.2172/1837809

Access: abstract record read (OSTI final report).

- **Claim:** Wound-field and hybrid-excitation synchronous machines with brushless capacitive power transfer for EV traction; lists advantages: no PM, easy field weakening ('infinite' CPSR in principle), high power factor allowing smaller inverters, lower high-speed iron loss, highest efficiency at high speed.
- **Method:** DOE project; machines plus capacitive rotor field exciter.
- **Machine / inverter:** WFSM and HESM, capacitive brushless exciter.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** See report body (not read).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Source for the WFSM/HESM advantages list and a third brushless exciter type (capacitive).
- **Limitations:** Report; numbers not read.
