# Huang2024novel

**A Novel Wireless Motor Based on Three-Phase Six-Stator-Winding PMSM** (2024), IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tie.2023.3301548

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Wireless motor based on a three-phase six-stator-winding PMSM whose WPT subsystem shares the main circuits of the drive.
- **Method:** FOC for the wireless motor; simulation and prototype.
- **Machine / inverter:** Six-stator-winding PMSM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 0.725 N m torque; 83 % transfer efficiency; 70 % mechanical conversion efficiency.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Shares stator inverter and wireless power path, like P3, but for a PMSM at tiny power.
- **Limitations:** Not pole changing; 0.725 N m.
