# Ayaz2023variable

**Tags: own-work**

**Variable Carrier Phase-Shift Method for Integrated Contactless Field Excitation System of Electrically Excited Synchronous Motors** (2023), IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tpel.2023.3300531

Access: abstract record read; open preprint exists (TechRxiv 10.36227/techrxiv.22189435.v1), full text not read.

- **Claim:** own-work. Contactless field excitation for EESMs that reuses the motor-drive inverter: the VSI's switching harmonics excite the WPT link while the low-frequency component drives the motor.
- **Method:** Variable carrier phase-shift method keeps the WPT input excitation voltage constant independent of the motor operating point; hybrid frequency detuning sets field current.
- **Machine / inverter:** EESM + its VSI (small-scale prototype), no extra high-frequency converter.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 100 V dc link, 60 kHz switching frequency; field current held near 5 A across modulation index and fundamental frequency; field current reduced by detuning the switching frequency.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Direct precursor of the P3 idea: excitation power taken from the inverter's carrier harmonics. In SPB each isolated-neutral coil group could host such a harmonic.
- **Limitations:** Small scale; unipolar field current; one three-phase inverter.
