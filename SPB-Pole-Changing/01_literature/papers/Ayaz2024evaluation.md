# Ayaz2024evaluation

**Tags: own-work**

**Evaluation of Possible Traction Inverter Topologies for Heavy-Duty Electric Vehicles** (2024), 2024 IEEE 9th Southern Power Electronics Conference (SPEC). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/spec62217.2024.10893180

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** own-work. Compares 3L-NPC, 2L-6-phase and conventional 2L-3-phase VSIs for heavy-duty traction using figures of merit and virtual prototyping (efficiency, power density, harmonics, reliability).
- **Method:** Virtual prototyping and efficiency maps.
- **Machine / inverter:** Heavy-duty traction inverter, 2L-6Ph vs 3L-NPC vs 2L-3Ph.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Qualitative in abstract; 3L-NPC gives higher switching frequency and smaller passives.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Baseline topology comparison by the same author; SPB is the next topology in this series.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only; no SPB.
