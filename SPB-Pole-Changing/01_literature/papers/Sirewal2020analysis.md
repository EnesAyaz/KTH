**Analysis of a Brushless Wound Rotor Synchronous Machine Employing a Stator Harmonic Winding (2020), IEEE Access**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2020.3017102

- **Claim:** Six-pole stator harmonic winding fed by six thyristors (no inverter) supplies a rotor harmonic winding + rectifier for brushless excitation.
- **Method:** 2-D FEA and experiment.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** Small machine.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Alternative excitation route needing an extra winding; SPB would use existing coil groups.
- **Limitations:** Extra winding and thyristors; fixed poles.
