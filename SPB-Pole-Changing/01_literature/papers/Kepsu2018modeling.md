# Kepsu2018modeling

**Modeling and Loss Analysis of 160 kW 30000 Rpm Bearingless Surface Permanent Magnet Motor** (2018), 2018 IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition (ECCE). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ecce.2018.8557366

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Loss analysis (2D and 3D FEM) of a bearingless SPM prototype of 80 kW at 30 000 rpm.
- **Method:** FEM loss analysis.
- **Machine / inverter:** 80 kW, 30 000 rpm.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 80 kW at 30 000 rpm (title says 160 kW).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Large high-speed machine loss example; mechanical losses not given in the abstract.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only; modelled, not measured friction.
