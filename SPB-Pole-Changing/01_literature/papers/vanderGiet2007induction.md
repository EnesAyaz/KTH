**Induction motor with pole-changing winding for variable supply frequency** (2007), 2007 IEEE International Electric Machines \& Drives Conference. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/iemdc.2007.383647

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Method:** Prototype tests.
- **Machine / inverter:** Dahlander IM, 2:1 speed ratio.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 2:1 pole ratio.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Classic Dahlander reference; SPB replaces the contactor switching.
- **Limitations:** Aircraft pump.
