# Seyedi2025comparison

**Comparison Between Pole-Phase Modulation (PPM) and Modulated Rotational Harmonic (MRH) Excitation in Nine-Phase Brushless WFSM (2025), IEMDC**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/iemdc60492.2025.11061165

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Compares two multiphase-stator excitation schemes on equal stator design and rotor copper; both give 7 % more torque than a benchmark IM; PPM needs a lower harmonic-to-main current ratio.
- **Method:** Nine-phase brushless WFSM FEA comparison.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 7 % torque over benchmark IM.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Latest state of the art for harmonic excitation of a WFSM through the stator; a baseline the SPB excitation proposal must beat on harmonic-current share.
- **Limitations:** FEA; fixed pole.
