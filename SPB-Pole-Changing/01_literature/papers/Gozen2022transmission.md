# Gozen2022transmission

**Transmission speed and ratio optimization for heavy-duty electric truck** (2022), Heliyon. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2022.e10028

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Transmission speed and ratio selection for a 27 t battery-electric road truck on Vecto routes; 2-, 3- and 4-speed designs.
- **Method:** GT-Suite 1D vehicle model, thousands of gear-ratio sets, regenerative braking, dual e-motor strategies.
- **Machine / inverter:** Dual e-motor e-axle.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 27 t. Running both motors at the same speed saves up to 5 % energy; turning one off saves up to 0.6 % (abstract).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Quantifies the energy value of matching motor operating point to load, a benchmark for pole changing.
- **Limitations:** Abstract read; 27 t not 40 t.
