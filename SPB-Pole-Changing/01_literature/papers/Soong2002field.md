# Soong2002field

**Field-weakening performance of interior permanent-magnet motors** (2002), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2002.803013

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Field-weakening comparison of axially laminated and multi-barrier SynRM and IPM rotors against a 2.2 kW IM baseline in the same stator.
- **Method:** Four prototype rotors tested in the IM stator; 50 Hz load tests at reduced voltage.
- **Machine / inverter:** 2.2 kW IM stator; SynRM and IPM rotors.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 2.2 kW baseline. IPM gives significantly better power above rated speed; multi-barrier IPM best.
- **Relevance to SPB:** IM field weakening is the weaker baseline, which motivates pole changing for IM.
- **Limitations:** Small machine; reduced-voltage test method.
