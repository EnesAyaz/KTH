# Libbos2025bumpless

**Bumpless Electronic Pole-Changing for Variable-Pole Induction Machines (TTE 2025, vol 11 no 1, pp 1201-1213)**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tte.2024.3401613

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Droop-control pole transition keeps shaft torque constant by ramping torque up in the new configuration as it ramps down in the old; flux transitions as fast as 200 ms.
- **Method:** Dynamic model of duration vs inverter ratings, simulation, experiment on 18-leg inverter with toroidal machine; compared with a ramp approach.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 18 legs, toroidal 36-slot IM; 200 ms flux transition; torque variation inside the machine's inherent ripple (abstract).
- **Relevance to SPB:** H06 for IM is already done experimentally; SPB cannot claim torque-gap-free transition as new for IM.
- **Limitations:** IM only; the rotor flux of a synchronous rotor cannot be handed over this way (field time constant); lab scale.
