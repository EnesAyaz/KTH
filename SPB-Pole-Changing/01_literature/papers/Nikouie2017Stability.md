# Nikouie2017Stability

**DC-Link Stability Analysis and Controller Design for the Stacked Polyphase Bridges Converter** (2017), IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TPEL.2016.2553129

Source: imported from Review-Paper-SPB/ref.bib (SPB lineage) 2026-11-02; DOI re-verified in Crossref. Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Capacitor-voltage stability analysis of SPB: balanced split of total DC-link voltage does not occur inherently; stability criteria and three balancing controllers.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** The stacked polyphase bridges (SPB) converter consists of several submodules that all are connected in series to a voltage source. The total dc-link voltage should split in a balanced way among the submodules. This does not always occur inherently. This paper presents an analysis of the capacitor voltage stability for the SPB converter. From the analysis, criteria for stability are derived and three alternatives of a suitable balancing controller are designed. The proposed controller alternatives and their associated stability properties are verified on an experimental setup and by simulation.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Fundamental: SPB DC split is inherently unstable in motoring (the origin of the H10 constraint). Balanced loads assumed.
- **Limitations:** no pole/phase-mode change, no hypothesis-level result for this repo unless stated above; check full text before quoting numbers.
