# Mallampalli2021six

**Six-Phase Pole-Changing Winding Induction Machines With Improved Performance** (2021), IEEE Transactions on Energy Conversion. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tec.2020.3009190

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** 6-phase electronic pole-changing IM gives 15 % more peak torque than the 3-phase 120-degree-belt pole-changing winding.
- **Method:** FEA and 1 kW prototype tests.
- **Machine / inverter:** 6-phase pole-changing IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 1 kW prototype, +15 % peak torque.
- **Relevance to SPB:** More phases (which SPB provides) improve the pole-changing winding factor.
- **Limitations:** 1 kW; starter-generator.
