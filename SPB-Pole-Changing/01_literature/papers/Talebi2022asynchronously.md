# Talebi2022asynchronously

**An Asynchronously Excited Brushless Wound Field Synchronous Machine (2022), ICEM**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/icem51905.2022.9910953

Access: abstract + first three pages of the full text read (UT Dallas lab copy).

- **Claim:** Single nine-phase stator winding with PPM produces a 6-pole synchronous harmonic and a 2-pole asynchronous harmonic; the latter induces current in a rotor induction winding, rectified into the field winding; self-start from zero speed.
- **Method:** 2-D FEA (Ansys Maxwell) of a nine-phase, 18-slot machine, 215 mm OD, 125 mm stack, 1800 rpm reference, 25 A/mm2, 90 Hz / 150 Hz; one nine-phase inverter.
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** FEA for a traction-class geometry; no power rating stated in the pages read.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Closest published form of H03: excitation power delivered by stator harmonic currents of a multiphase inverter. Uses a common neutral (common-mode currents of phases 1,4,7 make the 6-pole field), which an isolated-neutral SPB module cannot carry.
- **Limitations:** FEA only; fixed pole count of the main torque harmonic; saturation from the asynchronous harmonic offsets induction torque.
