# Walker2015dynamics

**Dynamics and Control of Clutchless AMTs** (2015), APVC, Hanoi. No DOI; URL in refs.bib. Access: full text read.

- **Claim:** In a clutchless AMT the shift consists of torque release, speed synchronisation and torque reinstatement; both vehicle jerk and shift duration depend on the whole process, not only the speed phase.
- **Method:** Simulation of an electric vehicle with a clutchless AMT (equivalent DC motor model, 200 N m demand, bump-function stage durations of 20, 200 and 400 ms).
- **Numbers:** a stage duration of 200 ms was chosen "with the goal of maintaining a shift time of less than one second"; 20 ms stage duration is the minimal case; 400 ms maximises shift duration and reduces peak jerk to about 30 m/s3.
- **Relevance to SPB:** gives the order of magnitude of designed torque-hole durations in a clutchless AMT (sub-second design target) for comparison with a pole-change gap.
- **Limitations:** passenger-scale simulation, not a heavy-truck measurement; no measured interruption times.
