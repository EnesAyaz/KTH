# Stella2024excitation

**Excitation SiC Converter for Electrically Excited Traction Synchronous Motor Using Rotary Transformer and Field Rotor Current Estimator** (2024), 2024 IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition (ECCE). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ecce55643.2024.10861444

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** 800 V SiC exciter with rotor-current estimator for an EESM with rotary transformer and rotating rectifier.
- **Method:** Estimator from average rectified primary current; simulation and experiment.
- **Machine / inverter:** 800 V SiC exciter, rotary transformer.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 800 V exciter bus.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Exciter electronics already at traction voltage class; a two-polarity exciter would be needed for coil-group reversal.
- **Limitations:** A rotating rectifier is unipolar, so reversing a coil group needs extra design.
