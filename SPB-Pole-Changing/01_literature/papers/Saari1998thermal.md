# Saari1998thermal

**Thermal analysis of high-speed induction machines** (1998), Helsinki University of Technology, Acta Polytechnica Scandinavica EL 90. No DOI (see refs.bib url)

Access: abstract + full text read (Aalto repository PDF).

- **Claim:** Doctoral thesis (HUT, 1998) on thermal modelling of solid-rotor high-speed induction machines; includes a literature review and measurements of air-gap friction and gas-flow losses and a thermal network.
- **Method:** Thermal-network model; friction coefficient from Bilgen and Boulos (Eq. 14 of the thesis), gas-flow loss from Polkowski (Eq. 15), measured friction loss P = k1 C_f ... (Eq. 18) in a high-speed machine; temperature rise within +-10 C of measurements in two high-speed motors; max power falls with speed to the power 2.1-2.2.
- **Machine / inverter:** Solid-rotor high-speed induction motors (20 000-200 000 rpm, surface speed above 150 m/s).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Bilgen-Boulos friction coefficients as in the Bilgen1973functional note; roughness coefficient k1 = 1 for smooth surfaces.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Source of the practical windage formula the analyst can apply to the traction rotor in the cap between rotor and stator, plus an example of calibration against measured friction loss.
- **Limitations:** Very high-speed small machines; not a 100-400 kW traction motor; thesis is HUT (Aalto), not KTH.
