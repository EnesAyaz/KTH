# Mughal2023integrated

**An integrated tribodynamic model for investigation of efficiency, durability and NVH attributes of gear mesh in electric vehicle powertrains** (2023), Tribology International. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.triboint.2023.108977

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Integrated tribodynamic model of gear mesh in EV powertrains: up to 30 % of contact load carried by asperities.
- **Method:** Analytical routines for mixed lubrication.
- **Machine / inverter:** EV powertrain gear mesh.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 30 % asperity load share.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Mixed-lubrication effect on mesh efficiency at low speed.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only.
