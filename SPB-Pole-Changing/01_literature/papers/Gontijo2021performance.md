# Gontijo2021performance

**Performance Analysis of Modular Multilevel Converter and Modular Multilevel Series Converter under Variable-Frequency Operation Regarding Submodule-Capacitor Voltage Ripple** (2021), Energies 14(3), 776. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/en14030776

Access: full text read (open access, AAU copy).

- **Quote (introduction):** "As lower the frequency of the synthesized AC voltage, more critical will be the submodule-capacitor voltage ripple since it is proportional to the capacitor reactance, which is inversely proportional to the current frequency."; at start-up of an MMC-fed machine "the peak value of the MMC submodule-capacitor voltage ripple ... can be four times higher than the nominal DC voltage of the capacitor".
- **Relevance to SPB:** the open-access statement of the standard 'low-frequency module capacitor ripple' mechanism: one fundamental frequency, ripple inversely proportional to frequency. Different from the SPB pulsation (two simultaneous spatial orders at f_a +- f_b in an isolated-neutral cell).
- **Limitations:** MMC and modular multilevel series converter under variable-frequency operation; no multiphase or pole-changing content.
