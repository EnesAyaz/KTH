# Volvo2022ishift

**Volvo Trucks' I-Shift is now faster than ever** (2022), OEM press release. No DOI; URL in refs.bib. Access: full text read.

- **Claim:** up to 30 % faster gear changing for the I-Shift automated manual gearbox; faster clutch activation gives a shorter torque interruption; faster sensors and microprocessor, an added disc in the gearbox brake, and smaller actuator air volume.
- **Numbers:** only the relative figure (up to 30 %); no absolute shift time or interruption time is given.
- **Relevance to SPB:** confirms an AMT truck shift has a torque interruption that OEMs still shorten; no absolute reference for a pole-change gap.
- **Limitations:** marketing claim, no baseline in seconds.
