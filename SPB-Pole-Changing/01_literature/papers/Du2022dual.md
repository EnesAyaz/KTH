# Du2022dual

**A Dual-Harmonic Pole-Changing Motor with Split Permanent Magnet Pole** (2022), Energies. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/en15207716

Access: abstract + full text read.

- **Claim:** Dual-harmonic pole-changing PM motor with split PM poles for electric tractors.
- **Method:** FEA of 8-pole and 24-pole modes with switch-selected winding.
- **Machine / inverter:** PM machine, 250 V bus.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 8-pole mode: 15 N m up to about 2750 rpm; 24-pole mode reaches about 16 000 rpm (8.9x base speed, 5.8x the 8-pole maximum).
- **Relevance to SPB:** A fixed-magnet machine can pole-change by using a flux harmonic of the same magnets, but needs electric switches (contactor-like).
- **Limitations:** FEA only; small torque.
