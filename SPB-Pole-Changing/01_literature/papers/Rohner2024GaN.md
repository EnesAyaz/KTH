- **Claim:** Compares a 3L flying-capacitor converter with an SPB of two series-stacked 2L converters for a 7.5 kW integrated motor drive with 800 V link.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** This article presents a comprehensive comparative evaluation of a three-phase Three-Level (3L) Flying Capacitor Converter (FCC) and a Stacked Polyphase Bridge Inverter (SPBI), specifically a converter system formed by two Series-Stacked Two-Level three-phase Converters (2L-SSC), for the realization of a 7.5 kW Integrated Motor Drive (IMD) with a high short-term overload capability. The 2L-SSC requires a motor with two three-phase windings and a split DC-link, but uses standard six-switch, two-level transistor configurations. In contrast, the bridge legs of the 3L-FCC feature flying capacitors
