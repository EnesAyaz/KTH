# Akagi1984instantaneous

**Instantaneous Reactive Power Compensators Comprising Switching Devices without Energy Storage Components** (1984), IEEE Trans. Ind. Appl. IA-20(3), 625-630. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.1984.4504460

Access: abstract record read; full text not read.

- **Claim:** instantaneous real and reactive power of a three-phase circuit are defined from instantaneous alpha-beta values for arbitrary waveforms, including transients (the p-q theory).
- **Relevance to SPB:** the textbook origin of the statement that, in a three-wire (isolated-neutral) three-phase system, voltage and current components at different frequencies give oscillating instantaneous power. For m = 3 the pulsation law is a corollary of this theory (gap-critic's 'textbook' objection).
- **Limitations:** m = 3 only; abstract only.
