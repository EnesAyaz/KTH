# Matsuyama2021design

**Design Study on High Torque Density Multiphase Pole-Change Induction Motor for Vehicle Propulsion Drive** (2021), 2021 24th International Conference on Electrical Machines and Systems (ICEMS). DOI: https://doi.org/10.23919/icems52562.2021.9634455

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read (IEEE Xplore returns an anti-bot page, HTTP 418, to curl even through the VPN; stopped for that host).

- **Claim:** Multiphase pole-change IM using harmonic operation of a multiphase inverter: 8-phase winding with span 3 switching 8-pole to 12-pole reaches torque density competitive with an IPMSM in a commercial EV.
- **Method:** 2D-FEA design study of phase number, harmonic order and span.
- **Machine / inverter:** Multiphase pole-change IM, mesh-connected windings.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Eight-phase, 8/12 poles.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Another IM pole-changing configuration by inverter harmonics; torque-density parity with IPMSM.
- **Limitations:** FEA only.
