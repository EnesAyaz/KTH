# Nikouie2016operation

**Operation under fault conditions of the stacked polyphase bridges converter** (2016), IECON 2016 - 42nd Annual Conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/iecon.2016.7793176

Source: imported from Review-Paper-SPB/ref.bib (SPB lineage) 2026-11-02; DOI re-verified in Crossref. Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Control strategy that lets the SPB converter survive a sudden single-transistor short circuit; stability under fault not studied before.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** The stacked polyphase bridges converter consists of several submodules that, on the input dc side, all are connected in series. Whereas controller designs presented in previous studies have been found promising for realizing equal voltage sharing between submodules, the survival and stability under fault conditions have not been studied. This paper presents a control strategy that enables survival of the converter after the occurrence of a sudden short circuit of a single transistor switch. The results are verified by simulations.
- **Relevance to SPB:** KTH SPB fault survival; the closest KTH precursor for H11 (post-fault of a stack) but about a short-circuit fault, not a mode set.
- **Limitations:** no pole/phase-mode change, no hypothesis-level result for this repo unless stated above; check full text before quoting numbers.
