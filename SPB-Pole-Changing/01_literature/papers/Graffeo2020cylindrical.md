# Graffeo2020cylindrical

**Cylindrical Wound-Rotor Synchronous Machines for Traction Applications** (2020), 2020 International Conference on Electrical Machines (ICEM). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/icem49940.2020.9270992

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Cylindrical round-rotor WRSM as alternative to salient-pole rotor in traction because of centrifugal stress on field conductors.
- **Method:** Analytical and FE comparison; preliminary mechanical checks.
- **Machine / inverter:** Cylindrical vs salient WRSM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** None in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** A round rotor with distributed field winding is the structure that supports several field coil groups, hence pole reversal.
- **Limitations:** Preliminary.
