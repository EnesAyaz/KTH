# Libbos2023winding

**Winding Layout Considerations for Variable-Pole Induction Motors in Electric Vehicles** (2023), IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tte.2023.3248444

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Compares toroidal, single- and double-layer distributed windings for variable-pole IMs; a low-aspect-ratio toroidal winding gives the largest torque-speed envelope at highest efficiency.
- **Method:** Generalised variable-pole design framework; toroidally wound IM on an 18-leg converter.
- **Machine / inverter:** Variable-pole IM, 18-leg converter.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 18 inverter legs in the experiment.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Quantifies the inverter-leg cost of variable-pole windings: SPB modules are the natural way to provide many legs at low voltage.
- **Limitations:** Winding emulated externally; no truck rating.
