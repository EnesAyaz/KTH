# Libbos2020modular

**Modular Multiphase Drives for Variable-Pole Induction Machines in Electric Vehicles (2020), APEC**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/apec39645.2020.9124085

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Generalised framework for the efficiency and sizing of a modular multiphase inverter driving a variable-pole IM; shows how variable-pole operation benefits and constrains the drive.
- **Method:** Framework + experiment: GaN 18-leg inverter, toroidally wound IM (UIUC, Libbos/Hao/Ku/Banerjee/Krein).
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** No ratings in abstract (companion TPEL 2022 paper: 890 VA, 6/4/2 poles).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Closest published statement of the core argument (many low-voltage legs + pole changing), but legs are on one common DC bus and are extra hardware relative to a 3-leg fixed-pole drive; no series stack, no truck.
- **Limitations:** Lab scale; 18 legs paralleled on one DC link; IM only; no drive cycle in abstract.
