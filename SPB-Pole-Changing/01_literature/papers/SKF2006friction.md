# SKF2006friction

**Using a friction model as an engineering tool** (2006), SKF Evolution. No DOI (see refs.bib url)

Access: PDF preview read.

- **Claim:** SKF's friction model splits bearing friction into rolling, sliding, seal and drag terms; M_total = M_rr + M_sl + M_seal + M_drag; seal friction can be a large share of total bearing friction for contact seals.
- **Method:** Qualitative description of the model and its physical separation; coefficient tables are in the SKF catalogue (not obtained).
- **Machine / inverter:** Rolling bearings generally.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** The explicit equations as used in practice are written out in Yan2024power: M_rr = phi_ish phi_rs G_rr (nu n)^0.6; M_sl = G_sl mu_sl; M_seal = K_S1 d_s^beta + K_S2; ball and roller drag moment M_drag formulas with V_M, K_ball/K_roll, C_w and the (n d_m)^2 d_m^3 terms. G_rr and G_sl depend on bearing type, size and load.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Bearing friction loss needs an actual bearing list; the SKF model plus Yan2024power equations give the structure.
- **Limitations:** No numbers in the Evolution article; no DOI; coefficient tables not obtained.
