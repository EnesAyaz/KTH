# Sakai2013permanent

**Permanent magnet motor capable of pole changing for high efficiency** (2013), 2013 IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ecce.2013.6647384

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** PM motor changes between 8-pole (PM torque) and 4-pole (PM plus reluctance torque) using d-axis current pulses.
- **Method:** FE analysis and experiment.
- **Machine / inverter:** Variable-magnetisation PM motor.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Core loss reduced 49 % (no load) and 33 % (load); speed range doubled.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Efficiency and speed-range gain from pole changing in a PM machine.
- **Limitations:** Needs re-magnetisation; small machine.
