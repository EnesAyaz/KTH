# Verkroost2024PowerDistribution

**Multi-Agent Control Scheme for Power Distribution in a Multi-Three-Phase Motor Drive Fed by a Stacked Polyphase Bridges Converter** (2024), IEEE Transactions on Energy Conversion. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TEC.2024.3405368

Source: imported from Review-Paper-SPB/ref.bib (SPB lineage) 2026-11-02; DOI re-verified in Crossref. Access: abstract + full text read (UGent PDF).

- **Claim:** Multi-agent control that exploits the power sharing capability of a multi-three-phase drive (agents need not contribute equally to the torque) while keeping the series-connected DC-bus voltage stably distributed, even if agents are not identical; 4 kW 15-phase axial-flux PMSM, motoring only.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** A multi-three-phase machine can be seen as a multi-agent system. An agent consists of a three-phase winding set of the multi-three-phase machine, fed by a dedicated three-phase power electronic converter, and steered by means of a dedicated three-phase controller. The series connection of all the three-phase power electronic converters to a single dc power source creates a so-called stacked polyphase bridges converter, and allows for the use of power electronic components with low voltage rating.
