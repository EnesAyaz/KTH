# Runde2020dual

**Design and control of a dual pole widths induction machine based on the Intelligent Stator Cage Drive concept (2020), ICEMS**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.23919/icems50442.2020.9290909

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** ISCAD (many independently fed stator bars) can run two pole widths and switch the pole number while operating, acting as a virtual gearbox in the traction drive.
- **Method:** Machine design and control method for dual pole widths (Runde and Gerling, TUM).
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** None in abstract (ISCAD generation 3: 300 kW at 48 V, Runde2019iscad).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Closest conceptual competitor: low-voltage, many-inverter, pole-switching traction drive; differs by parallel 48 V battery with huge currents, one bar per inverter, versus SPB series-stacked 1.2 kV.
- **Limitations:** Design stage; battery-fed 48 V parallel topology.
