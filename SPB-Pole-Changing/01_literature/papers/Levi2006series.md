# Levi2006series

**A Series-Connected Two-Motor Six-Phase Drive With Induction and Permanent Magnet Machines** (2006), IEEE Trans. Energy Conversion 21(1), 121-129. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tec.2005.853737

Access: abstract record read; full text not read.

- **Claim:** independent vector control of two machines (symmetrical six-phase IM in series with a three-phase PMSM) from one six-phase VSI through series stator connection (phase transposition); experimental proof.
- **Relevance to SPB:** two simultaneous currents of different frequency and spatial order on one inverter, separated by orthogonal planes; one common DC link, so no per-cell power pulsation is studied. It is the nearest 'two simultaneous orders' setting.
- **Limitations:** single inverter, not a series-stacked isolated-neutral drive; abstract only.
