# Chang2019improving

**Improving the Overall Efficiency of Automotive Inverters Using a Multilevel Converter Composed of Low Voltage Si mosfets** (2019), IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tpel.2018.2854756

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** A multilevel inverter of low-voltage Si MOSFETs improves driving-cycle efficiency and lowers cost versus IGBT and SiC-MOSFET two-level inverters.
- **Method:** Loss models of three inverters compared on a reference vehicle over several driving cycles, plus cost comparison and sensitivity analysis.
- **Machine / inverter:** Multilevel Si-MOSFET inverter vs IGBT vs SiC; passenger-car powertrain.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Abstract is qualitative: multilevel Si MOSFET has high efficiency and lowest cost; lower EMI, modular. Numeric values not extracted.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Direct evidence that low-voltage devices in a modular stack raise drive-cycle efficiency; supports the 75 V GaN rationale.
- **Limitations:** Passenger car, not heavy truck; single-star machine; Si not GaN.
