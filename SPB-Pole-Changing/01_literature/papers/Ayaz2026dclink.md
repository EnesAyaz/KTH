**DC-Link Voltage Scaling to 1.2 kV for Heavy-Duty EVs: System-Level Assessment With Experimental Validation of SiC Traction Inverter** (2026), IEEE Access. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3697246

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** own-work. System-level assessment of raising the HDV dc link from 800 V to 1.2 kV (battery, inverter, motor), with experimental SiC inverter validation.
- **Method:** Analytical first-order battery and motor assessment; virtual-prototyping inverter loss model validated on a 250 kW, 1.2 kV SiC prototype.
- **Machine / inverter:** 250 kW, 1.2 kV dc-link SiC traction inverter.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Model within ~10 % of measured inverter loss; ~56 % less cable conduction loss or ~33 % less cable mass at 1.2 kV; prototype efficiency ~99.12 %, 49.4 kW/L specific and 20.83 kW/L bounding-box power density.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Source of the 1.2 kV reference-system rationale and a 250 kW SiC baseline against which the 16 x 75 V GaN SPB should be compared.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only; PMSM-type machine assumed.
