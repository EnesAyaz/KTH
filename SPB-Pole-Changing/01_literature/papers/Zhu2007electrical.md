# Zhu2007electrical

**Electrical Machines and Drives for Electric, Hybrid, and Fuel Cell Vehicles** (2007), Proceedings of the IEEE. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/jproc.2006.892482

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Reviews IM, SRM and PM brushless machines for EV/HEV/FCV: high torque density, efficiency over wide range, high maximum speed; compares constant-torque and constant-power capability.
- **Method:** Review.
- **Machine / inverter:** IM, SRM, PM brushless.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** None in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Classic source for CPSR requirement and the field-weakening limit of PM machines.
