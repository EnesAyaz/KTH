# Umesh2016dual

**Dual-Inverter-Fed Pole-Phase Modulated Nine-Phase Induction Motor Drive With Improved Performance (TIE 2016, 63(4), 2036-2043)**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tie.2016.2561268

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Dual-inverter nine-phase PPM IM, 1:3 speed ratio (4-pole to 12-pole): phase grouping removes low-order harmonics, carrier phase-shift in 12-pole mode, higher dc-link utilisation.
- **Method:** FEM co-simulation and 5 hp nine-phase experiment (per search summary).
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** 5 hp, 9 phases, 4/12 poles.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Inverters grouped into three-phase sets act as modules; the closest PPM paper to SPB module grouping; but modules share one DC link and the motive is torque ripple, not low-voltage devices.
- **Limitations:** 5 hp; no stack.
