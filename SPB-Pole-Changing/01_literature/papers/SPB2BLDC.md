- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** Brushless DC operation with a trapezoidal waveform of permanent magnet machines provides a simple, robust and cost effective method to drive the motor. In this paper, the proven technology is applied to a motor drive system with series-connected multiphase windings and converter cells. The proposed topology provides a modular approach and enables MOSFET, which is more cost-effective and efficient, to replace the IGBT which is typically used in 3-phase motor drives.
