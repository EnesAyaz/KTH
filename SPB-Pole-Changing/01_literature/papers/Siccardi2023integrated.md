# Siccardi2023integrated

**Design and Optimization of an Integrated Modular Motor Drive for Low Voltage High Power Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor** (2023), 2023 25th European Conference on Power Electronics and Applications (EPE'23 ECCE Europe). DOI: https://doi.org/10.23919/epe23ecceeurope58414.2023.10264347

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Integrated modular motor drive (IMMD) for an open-winding PMSM at low voltage and high current; loss model validated experimentally.
- **Method:** Transistor + conductor loss calculation for electrical and thermal design; prototype.
- **Machine / inverter:** Open-winding PMSM, IMMD modules.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Max efficiency 97 % at 1.63 kW output; loss-model discrepancy 3.6 %.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Example of a module-per-winding drive with low-voltage devices.
- **Limitations:** Only 1.63 kW; PMSM so no pole changing.
