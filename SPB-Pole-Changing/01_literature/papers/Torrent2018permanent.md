# Torrent2018permanent

**Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor with Different Rotor Structures for Traction Motor in High Speed Trains** (2018), Energies. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/en11061549

Access: abstract + full text read (MDPI open access).

- **Claim:** PMSM rotor structures give higher efficiency than the induction traction motor of Spanish AVE classes 102-112 high-speed trains; recommended for implementation.
- **Method:** IM characterised against Renfe data, equivalent PMSM sized, FEA and equivalent-circuit efficiency at several operating points.
- **Machine / inverter:** IM traction motors in AVE 102-112 (8 motors, train power 8000 kW, 25 kV, max 330 km/h per its Table 1), versus SPM, RM-IPM and TM-IPM rotors.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Intro history: TGV Paris-Sud-Est 535 kW DC; TGV Atlantique 1130 kW wound-rotor synchronous; Eurostar 1020 kW induction; AGV 760 kW PMSM (2004). Nominal efficiency of the PMSM rotors 0.965-0.9725 pu, higher than the IM (IM value lost in the garbled table extraction).
- **Relevance to SPB:** Rail evidence: wound-rotor synchronous (TGV-A) and IM were both used, trend is now PMSM; IM efficiency is lower than PMSM at MW scale.
- **Limitations:** Simulation; table extraction garbled.
