- **Claim:** Method to transfer active and reactive power accurately between three-phase winding sets of a multiphase IM; sets do not share power as they share torque.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** This paper introduces a method which can accurately transfer active and reactive power between sets of three-phase windings of a multiphase induction machine. The machine has to have a multiple of three phases (3, 6, 9, etc.), with each three phases representing one set. The paper demonstrates first that the sets do not share the power in the same way as they share a torque. Therefore, the paper proposes a method that is capable of achieving accurate power sharing.
