# Potter2022experimental

**Experimental Analysis of a New Type of Harmonic-Excited Synchronous Machine with Special Consideration of the Core Losses (2022), ICEM**. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/icem51905.2022.9910845

Access: abstract record read (Crossref/OpenAlex, DOI resolves); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Prototype of a brushless harmonic-excited WRSM without auxiliary windings and with full torque at start-up; excitation power crosses the gap by a superimposed harmonic field of different pole-pair number, made by feeding parallel paths of stator and rotor winding separately.
- **Method:** Prototype with test-bench results, analytical core-loss model (TU Dortmund, Potter/Pfost/Schullerus).
- **Machine / inverter and numbers:** No numbers in abstract; the thesis (DOI 10.17877/DE290R-26088, abstract page read) names iron losses and high DC-link voltage demand as the penalties.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Experimental proof that stator-harmonic excitation works and costs core loss plus dc-link voltage; the SPB advantage is that voltage headroom per module is a design variable.
- **Limitations:** Fixed pole count; one (modified) converter.
