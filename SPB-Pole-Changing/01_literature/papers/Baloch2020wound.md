# Baloch2020wound

**A Wound-Field Pole-Changing Vernier Machine for Electric Vehicles** (2020), IEEE Access. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2020.2994064

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Wound-field pole-changing vernier machine with fractional-slot concentrated winding for EVs, operating in vernier mode at low speed and WFSM mode at high speed.
- **Method:** Topology, pole-changing method, FEA and manufactured prototype with experimental validation.
- **Machine / inverter:** Wound-field pole-changing vernier machine, FSCW.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** No numbers in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Prototype-validated precedent for WRSM pole changing by rotor field-set reversal (extends Baloch2019distributed).
- **Limitations:** Vernier machine, EV-scale; not SPB.
