# Yang2012electronic

**Electronic pole changing technique of multiphase induction motor based on vector control** (2012), International Transactions on Electrical Energy Systems. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/etep.1627

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Electronic pole changing (EPC) of multiphase IM by vector control, harmonic currents in different planes, no power-off.
- **Method:** Multiphase transformation theory and vector control; nine-phase IM experiment.
- **Machine / inverter:** Nine-phase IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Claims wider constant-power range without larger volume or current.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Experimental hitless EPC in a multiphase IM.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only; no ratings. Published online 2012, volume 23 (2013).
