# Uerlich2022experimental

**Experimental Validation of an Automated Approach for Estimating the Efficiency and Heat Balance of Gearboxes Based on an Electrified Heavy Commercial Vehicle Axle** (2022), World Electric Vehicle Journal. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj13080142

Access: abstract + full text read (MDPI open access).

- **Claim:** Automated gearbox efficiency and heat-balance model, validated on a prototype two-speed heavy commercial vehicle e-axle (Concept-ELV2): model deviation of efficiency about 5 % at 100 N m input and up to 10 % at zero torque; simulated temperatures within 1.5-4.5 C of measured.
- **Method:** Component-level models, test bench measurement of efficiency maps at input torques 100-400 N m, speeds 2500-7500 rpm, oil inlet 50 C.
- **Machine / inverter:** Two-gear heavy-commercial-vehicle e-axle: ratios 14.847 and 46.128, with a planetary stage; adding the planetary stage reduces efficiency by about one percent over the whole map.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Numeric efficiency values are in figures not extractable from text. The model reproduces measured efficiency within 15 % relative deviation over the map, worse at low load because load-independent losses are under-modelled.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Only heavy-commercial-vehicle e-axle efficiency validation found with measurements; shows low-load deviations are the weakness.
- **Limitations:** Absolute efficiency map not extractable; axle power not stated in the text read.
