# Buyukdegirmenci2014evaluation

**Evaluation of Induction and Permanent-Magnet Synchronous Machines Using Drive-Cycle Energy and Loss Minimization in Traction Applications** (2014), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2013.2266352

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read (IEEE Xplore returns an anti-bot page, HTTP 418, to curl even through the VPN; stopped for that host).

- **Claim:** IPMSM has higher efficiency for most conditions than a similarly rated IM over drive cycles, but with optimal-flux loss minimisation the IM efficiency becomes comparable to the IPMSM.
- **Method:** Machines of similar ratings in the same vector-controlled drive; drive-cycle energy and efficiency (not static maps); HEV simulator and hardware-in-the-loop.
- **Machine / inverter:** IM vs IPMSM, passenger HEV scale.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** No numbers in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Key published statement on the IM-vs-PMSM cycle efficiency gap and its closure by flux optimisation; supports caution about a claimed 97 % IM cruise efficiency.
- **Limitations:** Passenger-car scale, not 300 kW.
