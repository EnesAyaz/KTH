- **Claim:** Nine-phase machine with three isolated neutral points fed by three cascaded 3-phase VSIs (series-connected DC links); any imbalance in machine phases or inverters causes unequal power sharing between winding sets and unequal DC voltages; DC-link balancing controller with current/power sharing control developed, motoring and generating.
- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** This paper explores the possibility of using a nine-phase machine, with three isolated neutral points, in order to produce a high dc-link voltage by cascading three lower voltage three-phase voltage source inverters. Each inverter drives one winding set of the machine. Consequently, used inverter switches and capacitors have three times smaller voltage rating when compared to the single high-voltage dc link, a desirable feature in high-power and high-voltage drives, such as wind turbines or ship propulsion.
