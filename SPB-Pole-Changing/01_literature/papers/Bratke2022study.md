# Bratke2022study

**Study of Pole Changing of a Hybrid Excited Synchronous Machine with Stator Cage Winding** (2022), 2022 25th International Conference on Electrical Machines and Systems (ICEMS). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/icems56177.2022.9983196

Access: abstract record read; full text not read.

- **Claim:** Pole changing of a hybrid-excited synchronous machine with stator cage winding reduces core loss (frequency scales with pole pairs) and raises part-load efficiency; the energy benefit over standard driving cycles is positive.
- **Method:** Performance maps from response surfaces (avoids FE optimisation), operating points from a longitudinal vehicle model over standardised cycles, energy consumption via look-up tables.
- **Machine / inverter:** Hybrid-excited synchronous machine with stator cage winding (bars as phases), EV traction.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** No numbers in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Only found hybrid-excited pole-changing study that evaluates drive-cycle energy: closest precedent for the SPB drive-cycle question; stator cage with one inverter leg per bar is conceptually the many-leg limit of SPB.
- **Limitations:** Passenger-car cycles; abstract only.
