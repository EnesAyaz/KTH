# Hussain2022wound

**Wound Rotor Synchronous Motor as Promising Solution for Traction Applications** (2022), Electronics. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11244116

Access: abstract + full text read.

- **Claim:** A WRSM in the 2010 Toyota Prius IPMSM envelope is more efficient at light load and in the field-weakening region; PM motors are poor at low load.
- **Method:** 2D FEA with optimisation; compared with ORNL published Prius IPMSM data.
- **Machine / inverter:** 60 kW, 2768 rpm WRSM with Prius stator dimensions.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 60 kW rated, 2768 rpm, 191.46 N m average torque (7.5 % below rated), 120.5 V rms back EMF at rated speed, 178.5 A rms stator current at the test point, 0.97 % efficiency gain for the optimised model. Above base speed the field current and current angle are reduced.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Evidence that a WRSM weakens flux with I_f rather than -I_d, giving higher efficiency in the field-weakening region.
- **Limitations:** FEA only; passenger car; no inverter.
