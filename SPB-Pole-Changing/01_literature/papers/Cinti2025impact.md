# Cinti2025impact

**Impact of Rotor Excitation Current During Flux-Weakening Control of Hybrid-Excited Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor** (2025), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2024.3471977

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Flux-weakening control of a hybrid-excited PMSM acts on both stator currents and the rotor excitation current (feed-forward reduction of rotor flux); compared with an IPM of equal rated parameters.
- **Method:** Voltage-loop stator current control plus feed-forward excitation reference; simulations and experiments.
- **Machine / inverter:** Hybrid-excited PM synchronous motor (Padova, Bianchi coauthor).
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** No numbers in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Control reference for HESM field weakening; shows I_f replaces some -I_d.
- **Limitations:** Abstract only; no pole changing.
