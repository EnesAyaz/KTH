- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** Stacked Polyphase Bridge Converters (SPB-C) are well suited for integrated motor drives, i.e. for systems that combine an electric motor with its supplying power electronic converter in one compact unit. A SPB-C requires a motor with multiple 3-phase winding systems. In the literature, the SPB-C is typically combined with motors whose winding systems are phase-aligned. This type of winding is magnetically equivalent to a 3-phase winding (with parallel branches).
