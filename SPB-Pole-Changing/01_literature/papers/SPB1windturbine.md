- **Abstract (first 600 chars):** A novel wind energy conversion system (WECS) based on series-connected converter is proposed in this paper. In multi-phase series-connected converter, the de sides of three phase are series-connected to reduce the number and voltage stress of power switches and dv/dt at ac side. The insulation requirements for the generator is different from that of star-connected generator with two-level, three-phase inverter, due to the structure of series-connection.
