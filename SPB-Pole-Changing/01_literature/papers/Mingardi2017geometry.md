# Mingardi2017geometry

**Geometry of Line Start Synchronous Motors Suitable for Various Pole Combinations** (2017), IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tia.2017.2702581

Access: abstract record read.

- **Claim:** Same stator and rotor laminations can serve 2- and 4-pole line-start synchronous motors by rearranging the stator winding.
- **Method:** Design plus prototype tests.
- **Machine / inverter:** Line-start PM synchronous motor, 2/4-pole.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** 2-pole and 4-pole on one lamination.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Shows that stator rearrangement alone sets the pole count of a magnet rotor when the rotor geometry is pole-flexible.
- **Limitations:** Line start, small.
