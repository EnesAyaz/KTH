# Latif2021loss

**Loss Minimization Control of an Electronic Pole Changing 4-pole/2-pole Induction Motor** (2021), 2021 IEEE Energy Conversion Congress and Exposition (ECCE). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ecce47101.2021.9595843

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Loss-minimising pole-change decision control for a 4/2-pole EPC IM.
- **Method:** Simulink with Motor-CAD parameters; open-loop experiment.
- **Machine / inverter:** 4/2-pole IM.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** Smooth torque transition while minimising loss.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Gives the pole-change decision logic needed for a drive-cycle efficiency gain.
- **Limitations:** Small scale, simulation-heavy.
