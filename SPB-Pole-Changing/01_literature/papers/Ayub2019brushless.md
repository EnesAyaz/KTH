# Ayub2019brushless

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/tmag.2019.2893883

Access: abstract record read (OpenAlex/Crossref); full text not read.

- **Claim:** Novel winding scheme generating an additional sub-harmonic MMF with a single inverter and symmetric slots, picked up by a rotor harmonic winding and rectified to the field winding; FEA validated experimentally.
- **Method:** FEA plus experiment.
- **Machine / inverter:** Single inverter, symmetrical winding; notes existing SH schemes use a dual inverter or an asymmetric winding.
- **Power / voltage / speed numbers:** none in abstract.
- **Relevance to SPB:** Experimental validation of sub-harmonic brushless excitation; unipolar.
- **Limitations:** abstract only; small machines, FEA mostly; unipolar field current.
