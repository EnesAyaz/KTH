# Independent blind review: spb-bb-revb-dpt (2026-11-10)

Reviewer: independent agent, no knowledge of the project history.
Read: only `hardware/spb-bb-revb-dpt/` (board, project, rules, footprints, `fab/`) and the EPC2361 datasheet (rev. July 2026).
Not read: reports, simulations, scripts, logs, notes.
Method: the board file was dumped with the KiCad 7 `pcbnew` Python module. Distances, counts and plane cuts were measured with small scripts on that dump. Coordinates are KiCad board coordinates in mm (x right, y down).

Words used: **measured** = taken from the board or fab files. **Inferred** = my estimate or reasoning, not simulated.

## 1. What I think this board is

A double-pulse test board for one half-bridge leg built from GaN FETs. The silk says "SPB LEG REV B - DPT BOARD", "75 V DC - CHECK POLARITY" and "4 x EPC2361 PER SWITCH".

- Size 89.5 x 85.2 mm, 4 layers, 1.6 mm, 2 oz copper on all layers, 0.1 mm prepreg between L1-L2 and L3-L4, ENIG, filled and capped via-in-pad.
- Layer use: F.Cu = FETs, capacitors, shunts. In1 = DC- (net DCN) return plane. In2 = DC+ (DCP) and switch node (SW). B.Cu = more capacitors, connectors, test pads.
- Power stage: 8 x EPC2361 (100 V GaN, 1 mOhm class). They form 4 half-bridge cells side by side (silk CELL 1..4). Each cell has one high-side and one low-side FET.
  - Cell L1: Q1 (HS, y=104.9) and Q3 (LS, y=109.2) at x=116.75. Cell R1: Q2/Q4 at x=135.75. Cell L2: Q5/Q7 at x=164.25. Cell R2: Q6/Q8 at x=183.25.
- Each cell has its own 1 mOhm coaxial shunt (RS1..RS4, PMK UFCS type, y=112.65) between the low-side source (nets LSRC1..4) and DC-. The shunt is inside the commutation loop.
- Decoupling per cell: 6 x 0805 on top (y=101.1) and 4 x 0805 on the bottom, right at the high-side drain. Bulk: two rows of 2220 capacitors at y=88.05 and y=95.5.
- Two isolated dual gate drivers, 2EDF7275K: U1 at (126.25, 119.1) drives cells L1+R1, U2 at (173.75, 119.1) drives L2+R2. One driver output drives two FETs through a "star" with one gate resistor per FET. Each driver side has its own external isolated supply input with a common-mode choke, a zener split rail and a Kelvin return (nets KH1/KL1, KH2/KL2).
- Connections: DC+ J1 (144, 81.2) and DC- J2 (156, 81.2) at the top edge. AC output is one M6 bolt J21 at (150, 152.4) at the bottom edge. The silk says a copper bar goes from there through a LEM HO 250-S to the load.
- Measurement: 4 shunt outputs, gate probe pads and U.FL options, MMCX on a low-side drain-source, loop test points.
- Module 2 (x > 150) is module 1 moved by +47.5 mm.

## 3. Checked and found fine

- DRC report in the folder: 0 violations, 0 unconnected. I did not rerun DRC myself.
- Same-layer spacing between power domains (DCP, DCN, SW, LSRC, gate groups) with true pad shapes: minimum 0.20 mm on F.Cu and B.Cu, equal to the rule. At 75 V this meets IPC-2221B table 6-1 for coated outer conductors.
- EPC2361 footprint against datasheet p.11 and p.12: copper fingers 0.65 x 3.6 mm with 0.2 mm gaps, gate pad 1.102 x 1.14, mask openings and stencil apertures match the drawings. Paste is split into small apertures as the datasheet shows.
- Module 2 is an exact +47.5 mm copy of module 1 in the FET, gate and shunt area. 584 items compared per module; the only differences are the bulk capacitors (F4), J1/J2, the LED/bleeder D9, R39 to R41 and TP34 (F8).
- Gate star: track length from the star point to each of the two gate resistors is equal (5.09 mm each side, 0.3 mm track).
- DCN vias at each shunt: 39 per cell. DCP vias at each 0805 row: 8 per cell. In1 DCN plane is continuous under the FETs and shunt from y=102.65 to 117.65.
- Local driver decoupling: 1 uF 0603 directly under the output pins of U1/U2.
- Drill files against the board: PTH 0.25 mm x 44, 0.30 x 1106, 0.40 x 16, 1.00 x 8, 1.02 x 13, 1.85 x 8, 6.40 x 1; NPTH 2.70 x 4. All counts equal the board.
- Annular ring of plated component holes: smallest 0.26 mm (AC bolt stitching holes), rule 0.254 mm. Via rings 0.15 mm (0.6/0.3) and 0.10 mm (0.45/0.25).
- Smallest hole wall to hole wall: 0.26 mm, at (130.25, 110.5) / (130.15, 111.0), both net KL1. Smallest via pad to board edge: 1.35 mm.
- Gerber zip: all 15 files are byte-identical to the files in `fab/gerber/`. Only the two drill-map PDFs are not in the zip.
- `via_in_pad.csv`: 222 rows (190 in_pad, 32 near_pad), equal to the count in the fab notes.
- pos.csv against the board: every listed part has the right position and side.
- Mounting holes H1 to H4: NPTH 2.7 mm at the four corners.

## 4. Things I could not check

- Datasheets of the 2EDF7275K driver, the PMK UFCS shunt, the 2220 and 0805 capacitors (DC bias, ripple current), the Würth power terminals and chokes, MMCX and U.FL connectors. Web access failed during the review (usage limit) and I did not download files. So: driver isolation rating, driver pinout against the footprint, UVLO level against the 5.1 V rail, shunt footprint and power rating, capacitor voltage derating are **not verified**.
- Loop inductance values. I have no field solver here. The numbers in F1, F2 and F5 are hand estimates.
- Current sharing between cells. It needs a circuit simulation with extracted parasitics.
- The schematic against the copper, net by net. I read the netlist from the board file only.
- Temperature rise of vias and copper for continuous current. I only counted vias and squares.
- Solder mask webs and silk-over-pad, by measurement. I checked the EPC2361 footprint only. The fab notes claim webs of 0.20 mm or more.
- Whether the main BOM (Nov 5) and the JLC BOM (Nov 3) hold the same values. The main BOM uses designator ranges and I did not expand and compare them.
- The assembly PDFs and schematic PDF (not opened).
- Fabricator capability for 2 oz with 0.20 mm space, 0.1 mm prepreg, and 0.25 mm drill-to-copper.
- The mechanical fit of the 12 x 5 mm bar, the M6 bolt and the LEM sensor against nearby parts.
