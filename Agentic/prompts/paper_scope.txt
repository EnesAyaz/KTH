PAPER SCOPE

Working title:

Stacked Polyphase Bridge Converters for High-Power
Electric Traction Drives: A Review

Main objective:

Review stacked polyphase bridge converter architectures
for electric traction applications and compare their
advantages, limitations, semiconductor requirements,
machine requirements, integration potential, efficiency,
modularity, power density, fault tolerance, and control.

Main topics:

1. Conventional traction inverter architectures
2. Stacked polyphase bridge concept
3. Multiphase electric machine requirements
4. Semiconductor voltage scaling
5. Si, SiC, and GaN possibilities
6. Low-voltage GaN versus high-voltage GaN approaches
7. Integrated modular motor drives
8. Modulation and control
9. DC-link architecture
10. Common-mode voltage
11. Fault tolerance
12. Power density
13. Efficiency and losses
14. Thermal management
15. Reliability
16. Experimental demonstrations
17. Research gaps
18. Future research directions

Application focus:

- Electric vehicles
- Heavy-duty electric vehicles
- High DC-link voltage architectures
- High-power traction drives

Preferred publication style:

IEEE Transactions / IEEE review-paper style.

Target length:

Approximately 14-15 pages before references.

Important goal:

The review should not merely summarize individual papers.
It should systematically compare technologies and identify
research gaps.
