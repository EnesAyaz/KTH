\section{Bipolar OPT Excitation and PV-Cell Model}
Two stationary optical channels select the polarity of the bipolar field groups, while a third supplies fixed-polarity field groups. The supplied draft uses the single-diode cell model
\begin{equation}
I=I_{\mathrm{ph}}-I_{D0}\left[\exp\left(\frac{q(V+R_sI)}{nkT}\right)-1\right]-\frac{V+R_sI}{R_p}.
\end{equation}
The proposed matching expressions, transcribed from draft equations (4)--(7), are
\begin{align}
V^*_{\mathrm{MPP}} &= N_sV_{\mathrm{MPP}}-V_{f,\mathrm{return}},\\
V_{f,\mathrm{return}} &= V_{f,\mathrm{ext}}+I_{\mathrm{MPP}}R_{s,\mathrm{return}},\\
R_f^* &= \frac{N_sV_{\mathrm{MPP}}-V_{f,\mathrm{return}}}{I_{\mathrm{MPP}}},\\
P_{\mathrm{return}} &= V_{f,\mathrm{ext}}I_F+I_F^2R_{s,\mathrm{return}}.
\end{align}
