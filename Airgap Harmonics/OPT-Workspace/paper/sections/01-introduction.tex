\begin{abstract}
This work studies a pole-changing wound field synchronous machine supplied by bipolar optical power transfer (OPT) field excitation. The proposed architecture uses stationary optical selection to reverse selected rotor field-coil currents. The design couples the photovoltaic maximum power point to field resistance and rotor magnetomotive force. Electromagnetic and thermal validation remains to be completed and reconciled with the supplied draft.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
Wound field synchronous machines offer controllable field excitation, flux weakening, and a rare-earth-free rotor. Pole changing additionally requires reconfiguration of the rotor excitation pattern. The proposed system investigates bipolar optical excitation without rotor-side active switching or resonant compensation components.
