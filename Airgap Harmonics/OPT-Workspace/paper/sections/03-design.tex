\section{Machine Design Procedure}
The draft targets 10 kW at 3000 rpm in four-pole mode and 6000 rpm in two-pole mode, with a 400 V dc bus. Reported cell MPP parameters are 2.4 V and 12.5 A; the field winding has 24 turns and a stated 300 At MMF.
The design sequence is to specify operating points, determine the required rotor MMF, select PV parameters and series-cell count, calculate the allowable field resistance, and select winding and slot dimensions.
