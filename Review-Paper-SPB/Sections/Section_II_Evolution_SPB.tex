\section{Evolution of the Stacked Polyphase Bridge Concept}
\label{sec:evolution}
% ============================================================================

SPB belongs to the broader development of modular converter--machine
systems. Modular and integrated modular motor drives established
repeated converter--winding units, machine segmentation, power sharing,
and fault-tolerant operation \cite{Cengelci2000MMMI,Choi2011IMMD}.
Series-connected implementations extended these concepts to dc-voltage
sharing, enabling lower-voltage semiconductor devices
\cite{Wang2013GaNIMMD,Wang2015GaNIMMD,Wang2025GaNIMMD}. Other modular
drive topologies adopted the same principle
\cite{Wang2015SeriesModular,Ruseler2015InputSeries}.

Norrga \textit{et al.} introduced SPB in 2013 \cite{Norrga2013SPB},
followed by dedicated control and modulation methods
\cite{Jin2014Control}. Related series-connected drives demonstrated
internal dc-link regulation using machine-side degrees of freedom
\cite{Che2014SeriesConnected}. Subsequent stability analyses established
the balancing-controller requirements
\cite{Nikouie2015Stability,Nikouie2017Stability}, identifying dc-link
balancing as a central control challenge alongside semiconductor
voltage selection.

Research then expanded toward coordinated modulation, scalable control,
and converter--machine design. Modulation studies linked switching loss
to dc-link harmonics \cite{Jin2017Modulation}, while distributed control
provided an alternative to centralized balancing \cite{Jin2018Distributed}.
Comparative assessment against parallel-connected polyphase bridges
and other modular converters evaluated machine design, losses,
capacitor energy, cost, and redundancy, positioning SPB among several
candidate architectures \cite{Zhang2017ModularDriveComparison}.
Interleaving and phase-count studies further connected converter
performance to machine architecture
\cite{Verkroost2019Interleaving,VanDamme2019DCLink,Bringezu2020SPB,Verkroost2021Ripple}.

Recent work has extended SPB toward decentralized and reconfigurable
operation. Common-duty-ratio control, decentralized balancing, and
multi-agent power distribution reduce reliance on centralized
coordination \cite{Mocevic2023CDR,Verkroost2024VoltageBalance,
Verkroost2024PowerDistribution,Mocevic2024GateDelay}. Online module
isolation and reintroduction enable dynamic dc-bus reconfiguration,
including removal of a faulted cell without interrupting torque
production \cite{Verkroost2024Reconfiguration}. In parallel, gallium
nitride devices have renewed interest in the benefits of lower
semiconductor voltage classes \cite{Rohner2024GaN}.

Fig.~\ref{fig:spb_timeline} groups these developments by research focus,
tracing the progression from voltage partitioning to coordinated
converter--machine design and reconfigurable operation. The remaining
integration gap for electric vehicles is a unified, application-specific
SPB design framework that couples battery and dc-link requirements,
semiconductor selection, machine configuration, and control, evaluated
against common energy, power-density, cost, and reliability targets.
