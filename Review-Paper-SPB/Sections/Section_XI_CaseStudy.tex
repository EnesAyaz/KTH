Fig.~\ref{fig:epc2361_method} synthesizes the preceding review into an
iterative device-to-system design procedure. Electrical specifications
and the operating mission define the constraints; device voltage class,
cell count, layout, parallel count, capacitance, and switching frequency
are then evaluated together with the machine and cooling arrangement.
The verification loop revisits these choices until electrical, thermal,
and packaging requirements are met. It provides a framework for system
optimization, rather than demonstrating a global optimum.

The example in Table~\ref{tab:case_300kw} uses a 300-kW, 1200-V SPB.
For balanced sinusoidal operation,
$I_{\mathrm{ph,rms}}=2\sqrt{2}P_{\mathrm{out}}/(3MV_{\mathrm{dc}}\cos\varphi)$;
$M=1.15$ and a power factor of 0.9 give \CaseCurrent~A rms.
The EPC2361 is selected as an illustrative 100-V candidate
\cite{EPC2361Datasheet2026}. Sixteen cells operate at 75~V nominal;
maximum-bus operation, balancing error, and overshoot must still satisfy
\eqref{eq:semiconductor_rating}.

Four devices per switch use matched Kelvin gate paths and two half-bridge
drivers per leg. At 50~kHz, the updated screening model includes
temperature-dependent conduction, switching, dead-time reverse conduction,
and gate-drive losses. With a 0.5-nH commutation loop, 40-$^\circ$C ambient,
and a 0.65-K/W leg-to-air thermal resistance, it gives \CaseLegLoss~W per
leg and a junction temperature of 68.8~$^\circ$C. Four devices are the
smallest evaluated parallel count meeting the initial temperature and
voltage constraints; this screening does not establish a system optimum.

The revised bank uses twenty 22-$\mu$F MLCCs and forty 1-$\mu$F bypass
capacitors per leg. After dc-bias derating at 75~V, their effective
capacitance is 134.2~$\mu$F per leg, or 402.6~$\mu$F per three-phase cell.
The balanced three-phase switching-charge model predicts 2.85\% ripple
at 50~kHz under ideal shared-bus conditions, against a 3\% target.
Network-dependent current sharing, damping, and capacitor losses require
further verification.

The estimated efficiency of \CaseEfficiency\% includes semiconductor
and gate-drive losses only; PCB and busbar losses, capacitor ESR,
external supplies, control electronics, cooling auxiliaries, and machine
losses are excluded. Extracted layout parameters are listed in
Table~\ref{tab:case_300kw}. Assembly-level and experimental validation
remain necessary. The air-cooled thermal result does
not validate the shared-cold-plate motor-integration concept.
